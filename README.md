# 🌴 Welcome to Leonida

**Grand Theft Auto VI** takes players back to the neon-soaked streets of **Vice City**, but the adventure stretches far beyond the city.

The game is set across the wider **State of Leonida**, where beaches, nightlife, highways, wetlands, industrial areas, wilderness, and criminal activity create the backdrop for Jason and Lucia's story.

After what should have been an easy score goes wrong, the pair become caught in a much larger criminal conspiracy.

Their future depends on something simple:

### **They have to stick together.**

---

## ❤️ Meet Jason & Lucia

### 🔫 Jason Duval

Jason wants an easier life, but escaping his past isn't simple.

After spending time in the Army, he finds himself around criminals in the Leonida Keys. His relationship with Lucia eventually puts both of them on a dangerous new path.

### 🌹 Lucia Caminos

Lucia has learned to fight for what she wants.

She becomes Jason's partner at the center of GTA VI's story, with their relationship playing a major role in the events unfolding across Leonida.

> ❤️ **Two people. One dangerous situation. A whole state against them.**

## 🗺️ Explore GTA VI's World

### 🌆 Vice City

Neon lights, beaches, nightlife, luxury, crime, cars and the unmistakable atmosphere of GTA's famous city.

### 🌴 Leonida Keys

A tropical region filled with islands, waterways and a very different pace of life.

### 🐊 Grassrivers

Wetlands where civilization gives way to the wild side of Leonida.

### ⚓ Port Gellhorn

A rough coastal location with its own identity and problems.

### 🏭 Ambrosia

Industry and local power shape this part of the state.

### ⛰️ Mount Kalaga National Park

A more remote wilderness environment away from Vice City's crowded streets.
